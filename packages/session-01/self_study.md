# Session 01 — Self-Study Pack (SSP-01)

Content that supports L01 but is done on your own time. Est. ~6 hours across the week.

- **Hardware & boot** — CPU/RAM/disk, BIOS/UEFI, the boot sequence. Why it matters for forensics later.
- **Virtualization theory** — Type 1 vs Type 2, why the lab uses Type 2, snapshots and why analysts love them.
- **Windows editions & security features** — Home vs Pro/Enterprise; BitLocker, VSS, Defender, Firewall (read-level only).
- **CIA / AAA worksheet (LAB-L01-E)** — map ten everyday controls to Confidentiality, Integrity or Availability, and to Authentication, Authorization, Accounting.
- **Free rooms** — TryHackMe *Windows Fundamentals 2 & 3* (free), *Intro to Defensive Security* (free).

Deliverable for E: the completed worksheet, brought to L02.
